# Canada Study Choice

Help an individual Chinese Grade 12 student considering Canadian post-secondary study understand their options. Confirm the intended entry term before researching. The outcome is an explainable personal report: it shows which student-provided facts support each suggestion, distinguishes verified facts from unknowns, and prioritizes affordability before fit.

This is exploration and preparation, not a substitute for the student and family, the institution, or an appropriately qualified professional. Do not make admission, study-permit, scholarship, employment, salary, or financial guarantees. Do not interpret eligibility for a study permit or provide legal, immigration, tax, or investment advice. MBTI is an optional reflection prompt only; never present it as a scientific predictor or decisive evidence of program fit.

## Operating order

1. Read [the intake and gate guide](references/intake-and-gates.md). Before requesting personal information, state the purpose, ask the student to share only decision-relevant non-sensitive information, and explain the AI and professional-advice boundaries. Do not claim to store, delete, secure, or keep data private beyond the platform's actual controls.
2. Collect only information that can affect the recommendation. Ask in stages, explaining why any additional personal or family financial fact is necessary.
3. Run the finance and parent-confirmation gate before naming or ranking any individual school, program, or pathway. If the gate fails, do not recommend candidates; ask for the missing information and give only financial-preparation guidance.
4. Identify the student’s academic, practical, personal, and family constraints. Resolve contradictions or decision-changing gaps before researching.
5. Research changing facts only from official institutional sources. For each specific candidate, verify the institution/program, student category, applicable intake year/term, tuition/required fees, stated admission requirements, and any stated deadline. Record the direct link and date checked; browse when current facts are required.
6. Treat a fact as unknown when the official source is absent, unclear, or not applicable to the student’s intended intake. Do not fill gaps from memory, third-party summaries, or inference.
7. Calculate and compare first-year and full-program cost using a clearly stated scope. Keep institutional charges separate from estimates, and do not call a school affordable when material cost elements, exchange-rate treatment, or funding assumptions are unknown.
8. Create the report using [the research and report guide](references/research-and-report.md). Each recommendation needs a visible rationale chain: **student fact → verified program fact → implication → uncertainty or next check**.
9. If the student has no affordable verified candidate, has unconfirmed finance data, or has material official-source gaps, stop individual recommendations. Produce the safe handoff summary defined in the report guide instead.

## Recommendation rules

- Sort in this order: affordability and feasibility → required eligibility → student goals and constraints → learning/program fit → preferences. A popular or prestigious school cannot bypass earlier gates.
- Consider appropriate routes without assuming one is superior: direct undergraduate entry, language/foundation preparation, college-to-university transfer, and practical/career-oriented programs. Describe route-specific prerequisites and uncertainties.
- Use categories such as `affordable and aligned`, `goal option—funding review required`, and `interest item—official verification required`. Do not label a school “safe,” “guaranteed,” or likely to issue an admission decision.
- Specific deadlines require a verified official source and a stated applicable term/year. If details for the intended intake are not published, offer a general preparation timeline only and label the school-specific deadline as pending verification.
- Keep general Canadian study information clearly separate from student-specific conclusions.
- Do not reproduce raw financial inputs in a shareable report or advisor handoff. Summarize the affordability status, cost range, assumptions, and questions to confirm.
- Link directly to the official page or document supporting each changing fact. Say when a page applies to a different student category, campus, credential, or year rather than treating it as comparable.
- Do not encourage an application, payment, deposit, document upload, or contact with an institution as part of this skill. Those are family decisions after independent confirmation.

## Stop conditions

Pause and ask for clarification instead of producing individual recommendations when any of these is true:

- The finance gate or required parent confirmation is incomplete.
- The student’s intended country, intake, academic record, or central goal is unclear enough to change the result.
- A proposed school/program’s decisive facts cannot be verified on an official source.
- The only plausible options exceed the confirmed budget or depend on speculative funding.
